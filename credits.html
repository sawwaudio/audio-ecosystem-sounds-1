Credits — saww audio ecosystem

Every sound in saww audio ecosystem is a real field recording. Recordings are excerpted, looped, mixed and level-adjusted inside the app and in our videos and live streams; visuals are generated from the sound. Licenses: CC0 / public domain / CC BY (and CC BY-SA, which is not used in videos or live streams).
